## Session Overview
This presentation explores the foundational principles, architecture, and real-world impact of the Internet of Things (IoT).

### Key Agenda & Topics Covered
1. **Introduction to IoT**: Concept, interconnectivity of physical devices, and data exchange.
2. **Definition**: Formal definition of machine-to-machine (M2M) autonomous communication without human intervention.
3. **Architecture**: 4-Tier pipeline comprising Perception Layer (sensors), Transport Layer (MQTT/WiFi/5G), Processing Layer (Cloud/Edge), and Application Layer.
4. **Applications**: Smart Homes, Smart Cities, Healthcare Telemetry, Precision Agriculture, and Industrial Automation (IIoT).
5. **Demonstration**: Embedded video demo (`media1.mp4`) showing physical device communication and telemetry streaming.
6. **Future Horizons**: Convergence with Edge AI, 5G, and cybersecurity considerations.
