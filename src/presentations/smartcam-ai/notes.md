## Abstract
Discover how AI computer vision, physics-informed cutting algorithms, predictive machine learning, and direct shopfloor control unite to automate precision machining without human error. SmartCAM-AI eliminates the 4 to 12 hour CAM programming bottleneck by taking 2D PDF engineering drawings, extracting 3D solid geometry, and synthesizing collision-free G-code toolpaths.

## Key Takeaways
1. Eliminate 95% of CAM programming lead times from blueprint to machine.
2. Physics-informed cutting calculations (Kienzle equations) maximize tool life and eliminate chatter vibration.
3. Volumetric 3D collision prevention prevents spindle crashes across 5-axis machines.
4. Direct shopfloor orchestration via MTConnect and OPC-UA protocols for real-time telemetry.
